What this is

YL Imagination Squad Automation ("the automation," "we," "this app") is a personal, single-operator tool built by Yerik Linares. It generates short photography-education posts (image carousels and short videos) and publishes them, on a fixed schedule, to accounts the operator owns and controls: the Instagram Business account and the TikTok account both known publicly as @yl_imagination_squad.

It is not a public product, is not offered to other users, and does not accept sign-ups. The only account it acts on is the operator's own.

What it does with the TikTok API

The automation uses TikTok's Content Posting API, through the following scopes, solely to publish the operator's own generated videos to the operator's own TikTok account:

user.info.basic video.upload video.publish

No content is posted on behalf of, or read from, any TikTok account other than the operator's own.

Content and ownership
